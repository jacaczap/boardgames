Privacy Policy

VoteNMeet

Last updated: July 6, 2026

Overview

VoteNMeet is an app for coordinating group meetings: you create or join a group, vote on dates and options, and get reminders. Anyone can register and create a group; joining an existing group requires an invite link from that group. This policy describes what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address and password (you set these when you register)
  • Display name (first name / surname) you provide
  • Profile avatar (optional, uploaded by you)

Group & Usage Data

  • Groups you create or join, and your role in them
  • Meeting votes (your date and option preferences)
  • Content you create inside a group (e.g. game/option entries, descriptions, and images you upload)

Device Data

  • Push notification token (used solely for delivering notifications)

Calendar Access

With your permission, the app can add meeting events to your device calendar. No calendar data is read or stored on our servers.

Photos Access

With your permission, the app accesses your photo library solely for uploading images or your avatar. No photos are accessed without your action.

How We Use Your Data

  • Coordinate meetings — display votes, approve dates, select options
  • Manage groups — membership, roles, and invites
  • Send notifications — survey and meeting reminders, and alerts to group admins about reported content
  • Display your profile — name and avatar visible to other members of your groups

We do not:

  • Sell or share your data with third parties for their own purposes
  • Use your data for advertising
  • Track your location

Sharing & Visibility

Your name, avatar, votes, and the content you add are visible to other members of the groups you belong to. Groups are private: content is not public and is not shared across groups.

User Content & Moderation

You are responsible for the content you upload. We do not allow content that is illegal, offensive, harassing, or otherwise inappropriate.

  • Report: any member can report a game/option, a member, or a group from within the app. Reports are sent to that group's admins for review.
  • Block: you can block another user; you will no longer see their name or avatar.
  • Takedown: group admins can remove reported content and remove members from their group.

To report content or a user outside the app, contact us at the address below.

Data Storage

Data is stored in Supabase (hosted on AWS), protected by Row Level Security so only members of a group can access that group's data. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are not visible to us.

Data Retention & Deletion

Your data is retained while your account exists. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from Profile → Delete account (removes your profile, group memberships, votes, and uploads). You may also contact us for deletion.
