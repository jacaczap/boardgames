Privacy Policy

Planszówki (BoardGames Meeting Planner)

Last updated: July 3, 2026

Overview

Planszówki is a private, invite-only app for coordinating board game meetings among a friend group. This policy describes what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Data We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address and display name (provided during admin-created account setup)
  • Profile avatar (optionally uploaded by you)

App Usage Data

  • Meeting votes (date and game preferences)
  • Board game entries you create or edit (including uploaded images)

Device Data

  • Push notification token (used solely for delivering notifications)

Calendar Access

With your permission, the app can add meeting events to your device calendar. No calendar data is read or stored on our servers.

Photos Access

With your permission, the app accesses your photo library solely for uploading game images or profile avatars. No photos are accessed without your action.

How We Use Your Data

  • Coordinate meetings — display votes, approve dates, select games
  • Send notifications — survey reminders, meeting reminders
  • Display your profile — name and avatar visible to other group members

We do not:

  • Sell or share your data with third parties
  • Use your data for advertising or analytics
  • Track your location

Data Storage

All data is stored in Supabase (hosted on AWS), protected by Row Level Security policies. Only authenticated group members can access app data.

Data Retention

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting the app administrator.

Children's Privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

Updates will be communicated within the app or via notification. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
